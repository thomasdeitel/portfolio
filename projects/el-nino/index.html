El Niño and Pacific Ocean Conditions

DSC 106 · 2026 · Climate data visualization

For our group project, we explored how El Niño changes Pacific ocean and atmospheric conditions through maps, timelines, and regional comparisons.

Open the interactive Pacific dashboard

My contribution

I created the tropical Pacific sea-surface-temperature anomaly analysis using CESM2 historical climate data from the public CMIP6 catalog. The workflow classifies winters using Niño 3.4 anomalies and maps average warming patterns during El Niño winters from 1951 to 2014.

The broader group visualization combines sea-surface temperature, precipitation, cloud cover, and outgoing shortwave radiation. The modeled historical patterns are not a live weather forecast.
